Privacy

Last updated 3 August 2026

Your data stays on your device unless you connect Google Drive. Trips, expenses and DVC contracts are saved in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB). There is no WonderPlan account, no analytics, no tracking, and no server of ours anywhere — there is nothing for us to hold, because we operate nothing.

What WonderPlan collects

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no profile, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no advertising identifier. We do not know who you are, that you use the app, or that it exists on your device.

What you type — trip dates, hotels, amounts, DVC contracts — is written to your browser's storage and read back by the app. It never leaves your device unless you choose to connect Google Drive.

Google Drive sync, if you turn it on

WonderPlan can copy your data between your own devices through your own Google Drive. It is off until you turn it on, and the app is fully usable forever without it.

  • Your data goes to your Google account and nowhere else. The connection is between your browser and Google. We cannot read it, and we receive no copy.
  • It is stored in Drive's application data folder — a private area only WonderPlan can read. It does not appear in your Drive, and no other app can open it.
  • WonderPlan can only see the one file it created. The permission requested (drive.appdata) does not allow it to read anything else in your Drive.
  • Sign-in is not stored. We keep no long-lived Google credential — only a short-lived token that lives in the page and is gone when you close it, plus a note that you chose to connect. Nothing that could let anyone else reach your Drive is written to your device.
  • Disconnecting removes the connection, not the data. Your backup stays in your Drive until you delete it.

Third-party requests

Until you tap Connect Google Drive, WonderPlan makes no third-party requests at all. Everything it loads — hotel lists, price benchmarks, categorization rules, and its two typefaces — is served from the same place as the app itself. No CDN, no font service, no external host of any kind. You can confirm it in your browser's Network tab: every request is same-origin.

Google's sign-in script is fetched only when you tap Connect Google Drive, never on startup. If you never connect, it is never requested.

The app is served by GitHub Pages, which — like any web host — receives the ordinary request information needed to serve a page. That is GitHub's processing, not ours, and is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

If you do not connect Drive, there is no backup

Because everything is local, clearing your browser data or switching devices loses what you have entered. Private and Incognito windows do not keep anything either. Adding WonderPlan to your Home Screen protects it from Safari's automatic clearing of unused sites.

Deleting your data

Clearing site data for WonderPlan in your browser removes everything stored on that device. If you have synced, deleting the backup from your Google account's connected-apps settings removes the copy held there. Nothing survives either step, because there is nowhere else for it to be.

Children

WonderPlan is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this statement changes, the date at the top changes with it. The app is open source, so every revision is visible in its history.
